***Challenge***  
Analyze survival data from over 600 breast cancer patients (GBSG2 dataset) to identify prognostic factors and quantify their effect on survival time.

***Focus***  
Implementation of Kaplan–Meier curves for univariate survival analysis; Cox proportional hazards models for multivariate regression; exploration of hazard ratios; assumption checking and validation.

***Results***  
Kaplan–Meier plots stratified by clinical variables, estimated hazard ratios with confidence intervals, and a validated Cox model highlighting key risk factors affecting survival.

***Stack***  
R · survival · pec · ggplot2 · dplyr · R Markdown
